What We Do

OUR SERVICES

Five specialist service areas, each designed to deliver targeted investigative and analytical value to our clients across sectors.

01

BACKGROUND CHECKS

Our background check service provides comprehensive verification and screening of individuals and entities. Designed for employers, corporate clients, and legal practitioners, this service covers criminal record verification, employment history confirmation, academic credential validation, identity verification, and financial integrity assessments.

Each background check is conducted with meticulous attention to detail and full compliance with applicable privacy and data protection standards, ensuring that clients receive reliable intelligence to support sound decision-making in hiring, partnerships, and due diligence.

Employment Screening Identity Verification Credential Validation Due Diligence Criminal Records
02

FRAUD INVESTIGATION

FAIB's fraud investigation service delivers thorough, evidence-based investigations into financial irregularities, corporate misconduct, procurement fraud, asset misappropriation, and economic crimes. Our investigators apply forensic accounting principles alongside intelligence methodologies to reconstruct financial events, identify perpetrators, and compile evidence suitable for legal or disciplinary proceedings.

We work closely with legal counsel, compliance teams, and management to ensure that findings are properly documented and defensible in formal processes.

Corporate Fraud Financial Irregularities Asset Tracing Procurement Fraud Forensic Accounting
03

OPEN-SOURCE INTELLIGENCE (OSINT)

Open-Source Intelligence analysis involves the systematic collection, processing, and interpretation of publicly available information from digital and non-digital sources. FAIB's OSINT service builds detailed intelligence profiles on individuals, organisations, and entities using data from social media platforms, public records, news archives, corporate registries, and other open sources.

This service is particularly valuable for risk assessment, competitive intelligence, background profiling, and pre-engagement due diligence.

Social Media Analysis Public Records Intelligence Profiling Risk Assessment Digital Footprint
04

DIGITAL FORENSICS

Digital forensics encompasses the recovery, preservation, and forensic analysis of electronic evidence from computers, mobile devices, storage media, and network systems. FAIB's digital forensics service is designed to support both legal proceedings and internal corporate investigations, ensuring that digital evidence is handled in accordance with chain-of-custody requirements and international forensic standards.

Services include data recovery, deleted file retrieval, device examination, email and communication analysis, and preparation of forensic reports suitable for court or disciplinary use.

Data Recovery Device Examination Evidence Preservation Network Analysis Forensic Reporting
05

DATA ANALYTICS & RISK ASSESSMENT

Our data analytics and risk assessment service leverages advanced analytical techniques to transform raw datasets into meaningful intelligence. This service supports organisations in identifying patterns, anomalies, and trends that may indicate risk, fraud, or operational inefficiency. We apply statistical analysis, data visualisation, and risk modelling to deliver clear, actionable insights.

Suitable for corporate governance, compliance monitoring, financial analysis, and strategic planning, this service equips decision-makers with the intelligence needed to navigate complexity with confidence.

Risk Modelling Data Visualisation Anomaly Detection Compliance Analytics Strategic Intelligence
