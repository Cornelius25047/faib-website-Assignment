Our Story

ABOUT FAIB

A specialist firm dedicated to forensic analytics and intelligence services across Botswana and the wider Southern African region.

Forensic Analytics & Intelligence Botswana (FAIB) is a professional services organisation established to deliver rigorous investigative, analytical, and intelligence capabilities to individuals, corporations, legal practitioners, and institutions.

In an environment where information integrity and evidence quality are paramount, FAIB bridges the gap between raw data and actionable intelligence. Our team combines technical expertise with deep knowledge of the Botswana legal and corporate landscape to deliver results that stand up to scrutiny.

We operate with an unwavering commitment to confidentiality, accuracy, and professional ethics. Every engagement is treated with the gravity it deserves — whether it involves a background verification, a complex fraud investigation, or a large-scale data analytics project.

FAIB serves clients across diverse sectors including corporate, legal, financial, government, and private individual markets, providing bespoke solutions designed to address specific intelligence and investigative needs.

  • Corporate & Business Clients
  • Legal Practitioners & Law Firms
  • HR Departments & Compliance Officers
  • Private Individuals & Entrepreneurs
  • Government & Institutional Bodies

Our Focus Area

Forensic analytics, open-source intelligence, digital evidence, fraud investigation, and data-driven risk assessment for the Botswana market and beyond.

Geographic Reach

Primarily Gaborone, Francistown, and urban Botswana, with capacity to serve clients across the SADC region.

Our Commitment

Every case is handled with professional discretion, methodological rigour, and a commitment to delivering evidence-based outcomes.
